1. Who We Are

F.A.S. (Forensic Auditing Services) is a compliance auditing platform built and operated by ALS Consulting Services. F.A.S. helps employers detect and document potential bias in automated and human rejection decisions. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how long we keep it.

F.A.S. is a business-to-business platform. Our direct customers are employers ("client organizations"). Job candidates whose rejection data is processed through F.A.S. are not our customers — see Section 6 for how candidate rights are handled.

2. What Data We Collect

2.1 Candidate & Rejection Data

F.A.S. receives rejection payloads from a client organization's Applicant Tracking System (ATS) via a webhook the client configures and controls. This may include: candidate ID, job title, rejection reason, employment gap flags, keyword-match flags, years of experience, education level, and protected-class demographic category where the client's ATS already collects it (e.g., EEO-1 self-identification data).

F.A.S. does not collect data directly from job candidates. Every candidate data point in our system arrives through a client-controlled ATS integration. We do not operate a public-facing intake where candidates submit information to us directly.

2.2 Reviewer & Account Data

For client organization staff (HR leads, compliance officers, reviewers) who log into the F.A.S. dashboard, we collect: name, work email, authentication credentials, role assignment, and a log of in-platform actions (decisions, overrides, escalations) tied to that account.

2.3 Website & Form Data

When you submit a contact form or schedule a call through this marketing site, we collect the information you provide (name, email, company, message). Form submissions are processed through Netlify Forms; call scheduling is handled through Calendly.

3. How We Use Data

We use the data described above to: run the forensic audit and disparate-impact analysis a client has engaged us to perform, maintain an immutable audit trail of reviewer decisions for compliance purposes, respond to inquiries submitted through this site, and schedule discovery calls.

We do not sell candidate or client data. We do not use candidate data for advertising, and we do not use it to train third-party AI models — see Section 4 for how our AI classification vendor handles inputs.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

F.A.S. relies on the following subprocessors to operate. A full technical disclosure, including data-residency and security certifications, is maintained at the Subprocessors page.

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and row-level access control for the F.A.S. platform.
  • Netlify — static site hosting and marketing-site form capture.
  • GitHub — source code repository. No candidate or personal data is stored here.
  • Calendly — call scheduling for discovery calls booked through this site.
  • ALS Consulting (offline backup) — encrypted local backup of audit trails and decision logs, maintained at our Winston-Salem, NC facility for chain-of-custody and disaster recovery purposes.
Planned: F.A.S.'s rejection-reason classifier is designed to use Anthropic's Claude API to convert free-text ATS rejection reasons into structured categories. This is not yet deployed and is not currently processing any data. When it goes live, it will run under a bounded, deterministic classification prompt — not open-ended generation — with every output subject to human review, and it will be added here and to our Subprocessors page as an active subprocessor at that time, with a Data Processing Agreement executed beforehand.
We will provide 30 days' advance notice before adding or replacing a subprocessor with access to client data, consistent with the change policy on our Subprocessors page.

5. Data Retention

F.A.S. retains audit records, rejection payloads, and reviewer actions for 4 years from the date of creation or the underlying employment action — meeting or exceeding the current retention minimum in every jurisdiction we are aware clients operate in. Where a discrimination charge or regulatory inquiry is filed, affected records are retained until final disposition, regardless of the 4-year baseline.

If a client organization terminates its agreement with ALS Consulting, its data is retained for 90 days to allow export, then securely deleted unless a longer retention period is required by law or an active regulatory matter.

Because candidate and employee data is held for this full 4-year window, the security controls in Section 7 — encryption, hashing, and role-based access — apply for the entire retention period, not just at intake. Data does not sit unprotected as it ages.

6. Candidate Data Rights

Because candidate data reaches F.A.S. only through a client organization's ATS integration, F.A.S. acts as a data processor on behalf of the client, not as the controller of candidate data. If you are a job candidate and want to access, correct, or request deletion of your data, that request must go through the employer you applied to — they control the underlying record and are responsible for candidate-facing data rights under applicable law (including GDPR Article 15–17 for EU candidates and analogous state laws).

We do not maintain a public intake channel for candidates to contact F.A.S. directly, because we do not hold the authoritative candidate record — the client employer does.

7. Data Security

Data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). Candidate identifiers are hashed before storage. Access to client data within F.A.S. is restricted by role-based permissions enforced through Supabase Row Level Security, so reviewers only see data scoped to their organization and role.

These protections are not limited to the point of collection — they apply continuously for as long as we hold the data, including throughout the retention period described in Section 5. Encryption and access controls do not lapse or weaken as a record ages.

8. Cookies & Tracking

This site does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or behavioral-tracking scripts. The F.A.S. dashboard uses essential session cookies/local storage required for authentication (Supabase Auth) — these are necessary for the platform to function and are not used for tracking across other sites.

9. International Data Transfers

Primary infrastructure runs in the United States. For EU-based candidate data, processing complies with GDPR Article 6 (lawfulness of processing) and Article 21 (right to object). Standard Contractual Clauses are available on request for clients requiring them.

10. Children's Privacy

F.A.S. is a business platform intended for use by employers and their staff. It is not directed at individuals under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from minors.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Client organizations will be notified of material changes affecting their data via email to their primary account contact.
