The Problem

Companies using AI to screen candidates face a critical compliance gap: AI systems reject candidates in seconds, but compliance reviews happen weeks or months later — if at all. By then, the liability is done. Regulators under NYC LL144, Colorado SB205, Illinois AIVIA, California AB 2930, the EU AI Act, and EEOC / OFCCP guidelines are already investigating. Your organization needs to audit before rejections go out, not after they've created liability.

How F.A.S. Works

F.A.S. analyzes every AI-driven rejection in real-time using forensic auditing. Our system flags high-risk decisions, forces human review before rejections are finalized, and documents the entire decision trail for regulatory defense.

The 7-Lane Audit Framework

Every ATS rejection is evaluated across seven forensic lanes simultaneously. A flag in any lane pauses the rejection and routes it for human review.

Lane 1

Age Proxy Signals

Detects language that indirectly filters by age — graduation year references, "digital native" requirements, and date-coded experience thresholds.

Lane 2

Employment Gap Penalties

Identifies ATS scoring patterns that penalize non-linear career paths, caregiving gaps, or periods of self-employment.

Lane 3

Disparate Impact Patterns

Flags requirements with documented statistical bias against protected classes under EEOC and OFCCP disparate impact standards.

Lane 4

Credential Inflation

Detects degree or certification requirements that exceed documented role necessity — a leading source of disparate impact liability.

Lane 5

Keyword Mismatch

Catches ATS rejections driven by missing synonyms rather than missing skills — where candidate vocabulary doesn't match the job description's ATS terms.

Lane 6

Disability Exclusion Language

Identifies requirements or phrasing that screen out candidates with protected disabilities under the ADA and equivalent international frameworks.

Lane 7

Legal Framework Triggers

Detects patterns that activate specific regulatory obligations under NYC LL144, Colorado SB205, Illinois AIVIA, California AB 2930, EU AI Act, and Texas TRAIGA.
