The Process

How F.A.S. Works

Five steps between an ATS rejection and a legally defensible outcome.

1

ATS Flags Rejection

Your applicant tracking system identifies a candidate for rejection. F.A.S. intercepts before execution.

2

Forensic Audit Runs

Claude analyzes the decision for proxy discrimination, 4/5ths rule violations, keyword mismatch, and employment gap bias.

3

Risk Score Assigned

Every rejection receives a LOW, MEDIUM, or HIGH bias risk classification with documented findings.

4

Human Override Gate

No rejection executes without documented human justification. HIGH-risk decisions escalate to a Compliance Officer.

5

Audit Trail Created

Court-ready documentation of every decision — AI analysis, human review, justification provided, and outcome.
