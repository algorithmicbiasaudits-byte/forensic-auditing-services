HOW IT WORKS

Five Compliance Flags.
One Decision Gate.

F.A.S. integrates with your ATS via webhook. When a rejection is generated, FAS HITL is notified in near-real-time — runs a forensic audit across five compliance flags — and holds high-risk decisions open, unresolved and undocumented, until a compliance officer provides documented justification.

FLAG 01
Proxy Discrimination Signal
Detects screening criteria and rejection language functioning as proxies for protected-class discrimination — age-related signals (experience ceilings, "overqualified" framing) under the ADEA, and employment-gap or credential signals correlated with disability, caregiving, or military status under Title VII and ADA.
HIGH RISK TRIGGER
FLAG 02
Disparate Impact Pattern
Applies the EEOC 4/5ths rule to flag rejection patterns that disproportionately exclude protected groups beyond the adverse impact threshold.
HIGH RISK TRIGGER
FLAG 03
Legal Trigger Language
Flags which regulatory frameworks apply to each decision — NYC LL144, Colorado SB189, Illinois HB 3773, California's FEHA/CPPA regulations, EU AI Act — and what documentation is required for compliance.
HIGH RISK TRIGGER
FLAG 04
Employment Gap Penalty
Evaluates whether gap-based rejections are individualized and job-related, or systematic — flagging decisions that penalize protected leave without inquiry.
MODERATE RISK
FLAG 05
Keyword Mismatch
Identifies cases where ATS rejection reasons are inconsistent with actual job requirements — surfacing algorithmic over-rejection before it becomes a pattern.
MODERATE RISK
