F.A.S. FORENSIC AUDIT ENGINE — RUNNING
!
Age Proxy Discrimination Detected
Rejection language contains age proxy signals: "overqualified," excessive experience framing, and "culture fit" language are documented proxies for age discrimination under the Age Discrimination in Employment Act (ADEA). Candidates with 7+ years experience are disproportionately flagged by ATS systems trained on junior role data. High litigation risk.
!
Proxy Discrimination Detected
Employment gap language ("inconsistent history") is a documented proxy for caregiving leave, medical leave, and military service — all protected under FMLA and ADA. High correlation with disproportionate impact on women and disabled candidates.
!
Disparate Impact Risk — 4/5ths Rule
Rejection based on employment gaps without individualized assessment fails the 4/5ths rule analysis. EEOC enforcement precedent: gap-based screening disproportionately excludes protected groups at a rate exceeding the adverse impact threshold.
~
Keyword Mismatch — Moderate Risk
Candidate skills (Supply Chain, Six Sigma, SAP) are directly relevant to the posted role requirements. ATS rejection reason does not reference a specific skill or qualification gap — suggesting algorithmic over-rejection.
!
Legal Framework Triggers
NYC LL144: Automated employment decision tool used without bias audit disclosure. Colorado SB205 (enforcement paused, pending court ruling & SB 26-189 legislative replacement): Gap-based rejection without human review violates individualized assessment requirement. EU AI Act: High-risk AI system decision without human oversight. OFCCP: Federal contractors must document adverse impact analysis and maintain records of all selection decisions under the Internet Applicant Rule.
~
Employment Gap Analysis
7 years total experience with unspecified gap detected. Gap duration and reason unknown — ATS did not capture context. Penalizing candidates for undocumented gaps without inquiry violates EEOC best practice guidance (2022).
!
Federal Contractor Compliance — OFCCP
Federal contractors and subcontractors are required under OFCCP regulations to maintain adverse impact documentation, track Internet Applicants, and ensure selection decisions are free from disparate impact by race, sex, and ethnicity. AI-driven rejections without documented human review violate these obligations and expose the organization to OFCCP audit findings.
✓
Comparative Assessment — Insufficient Data
No comparative candidate data provided by ATS. Cannot confirm selected candidate was demonstrably more qualified on job-related criteria. Human review required before rejection is finalized.
