3. Participant Roles & Responsibilities

Participating organizations must designate the following roles:

Primary HR/Talent Lead: Day-to-day system administrator and primary point of contact.
Compliance Officer or Legal Representative: Reviews and approves/overrides high-risk rejection decisions.
Executive Sponsor (CFO or CEO): Final approval authority on escalated compliance decisions.

IT Administrator or Technical Lead: Responsible for configuring the ATS webhook integration, managing API key security, and coordinating with ALS Consulting during technical onboarding. This role may be filled by an internal IT staff member or a designated third-party IT vendor contracted by the participating organization. If no internal IT resource is available, the participant is responsible for engaging qualified outside technical support at their own expense. ALS Consulting provides technical documentation and onboarding support but does not perform ATS configuration on behalf of participants.

All team members must be current employees or authorized contractors of your organization and have authorization to make hiring and compliance decisions on behalf of your company.

4. Rejection Justification Requirements & Business Necessity

F.A.S. flagged rejections require documented human review and justification. Justifications must include:

Job-Related Reason: Specific skills, experience, or qualifications the candidate lacks that are essential to the role.
Comparative Assessment: Evidence that the selected candidate was better qualified on the same criteria.
Job Analysis Documentation: Evidence that the stated requirements are truly job-related and consistent with business necessity.

Rejections lacking clear, job-relatedness-based justification may be flagged for human review override by the F.A.S. compliance team.

4.4 Participant Responsibility for Business Necessity

F.A.S. provides analytical decision support and flags potential compliance risks. F.A.S. does not validate, certify, or endorse the job-relatedness of any qualification standard, job description, or selection criterion. The participating organization remains solely responsible for establishing and documenting business necessity under Title VII, the Uniform Guidelines on Employee Selection Procedures, and applicable state and local law.

5. Human Review Accountability

All human decisions are logged with reviewer identity, timestamp, decision, and justification. F.A.S. monitors individual reviewer patterns to detect bias signals including disproportionate override rates for specific candidate groups, consistent rejection of candidates with employment gaps, systematic deviation from policy, and any patterns inconsistent with stated job requirements.

Quarterly reviews of reviewer decisions may result in compliance coaching or process adjustment recommendations.

6. Data & Privacy

Your Data is Confidential: We do not sell your data. All candidate and organizational data is encrypted at rest and in transit. Data is retained for audit compliance purposes and may be accessed by authorized ALS Consulting staff only.

Candidate Anonymization: Candidate identifiers are hashed and anonymized in our audit database. No personally identifiable information is stored without explicit consent.

Regulatory Compliance: F.A.S. maintains audit logs and decision trails compliant with NYC LL144, Colorado SB205, Illinois AIVIA, EU AI Act, EEOC, OFCCP, and Title VII documentation standards.

GDPR & Data Residency: For EU-based candidate data, processing complies with GDPR Article 6 (lawfulness) and Article 21 (individual rights). You agree to provide candidates with notice of AI use in hiring decisions as required by law.

Subprocessors: A current list of subprocessors engaged by ALS Consulting to deliver F.A.S. services is maintained at forensicauditingservices.com/subprocessors and is incorporated by reference into these Terms.

In the event of a suspected or confirmed unauthorized access to, disclosure of, or destruction of participant data (a "Security Incident"), ALS Consulting shall notify the participant's designated Primary HR/Talent Lead and Compliance Officer within 72 hours of discovery. Notification shall include: (a) the nature of the Security Incident; (b) the categories and approximate number of affected candidates; (c) the likely consequences; and (d) measures taken or proposed to address the incident.

6A.2 Participant Cooperation

The participant shall cooperate with ALS Consulting in any investigation, regulatory notification, or candidate communication required by applicable law. Where the participant is legally required to notify affected candidates or regulators, ALS Consulting shall provide reasonable assistance at the participant's expense.
