PRODUCT
01
How is F.A.S. different from other compliance tools?
Click to see answer
ANSWER
Most audit tools review decisions in aggregate, long after the fact. FAS HITL catches every automated rejection in near-real-time, runs a forensic audit immediately, and holds high-risk decisions open — unresolved and undocumented — until a human reviews and justifies them. That's mandatory, continuous review on every decision — not a periodic statistical scorecard. (FAS Auditing, our base engine, surfaces the same exposure math without the continuous human-review layer.)

How long does it take to get audit results?
Click to see answer
ANSWER
Under 3 seconds for the automated audit itself — the forensic analysis runs the moment your ATS generates a rejection and flags it immediately. If it's flagged high-risk, the decision is then held in your Compliance Officer's review queue until they document a decision — that step depends on your team's own turnaround, not an automated timer.

05
Are the audit results legally defensible?
Click to see answer
ANSWER
F.A.S. produces a court-ready audit trail for every decision — deterministic audit findings, human review, justification provided, reviewer identity, and timestamp. We recommend consulting employment counsel on high-risk decisions. F.A.S. results are analytical and provide decision support, not legal advice.

06B
Does F.A.S. support OFCCP federal contractor compliance?
Click to see answer
ANSWER
Yes. OFCCP requires federal contractors to document adverse impact analysis, track Internet Applicants, and maintain records of all selection decisions by race, sex, and ethnicity. FAS HITL runs disparate impact analysis on every rejection, forces documented human review, and generates a court-ready audit trail — satisfying OFCCP's core documentation requirements.

06
What if our team disagrees with a F.A.S. flag?
Click to see answer
ANSWER
Your team has full override authority. FAS HITL flags and blocks — it does not make the final decision. Your Compliance Officer can approve, override, or escalate any flagged rejection. The key requirement is that the decision is documented with a job-related justification.

Is our candidate data secure? Who can see it?
Click to see answer
ANSWER
All candidate data is encrypted at rest and in transit. Candidate identifiers are anonymized in our audit database — we never store names or personal details without consent. Only authorized ALS Consulting staff can access your data. We never sell your data.

What data does F.A.S. send and store?
Click to see answer
ANSWER
F.A.S. receives anonymized rejection data from your ATS — job title, rejection reason, years of experience, education level, and skills. No names, addresses, or demographic data are transmitted. All data is stored encrypted and retained for audit compliance purposes only.

Click to see answer
ANSWER
